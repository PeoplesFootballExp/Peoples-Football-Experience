
## Match Player

The player entity during a match is different from the one in the world of football. During a match, there are some information that can be ignored about the player and some new information that must be tracked. For example, during a match, it is not important to know the exact coordinates of where the player was born, perhaps just the country. Also, new statistics like minutes played need to be tracked.
